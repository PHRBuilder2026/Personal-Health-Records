Features

A comprehensive personal health record — 18 modules to manage every aspect of your health.

Patient Information

Your central health profile with personal details, emergency contacts, blood type, and more. Supports multiple countries with adaptive address and phone formatting.

  • Complete personal health profile
  • Emergency contact information
  • Multi-country address support (US, Canada, UK, Australia, and other English-speaking countries)
  • Additional language support coming in future updates
  • Privacy controls to blur sensitive fields on screen and exclude them from printed reports
  • Up to 10 patient records per device — designed for personal and family use

Import from Patient Portals (CCDA)

Most patient portals — MyChart, Athenahealth, Cerner, eClinicalWorks, and others — let you download a Continuity of Care Document (CCDA). PHR reads that file and pulls your data into the right modules, so you don't have to retype anything.

  • One importer for each section: Lab Results, Medications, Conditions, Allergies, Immunizations, Procedures, Implants, Imaging Reports, Patient Info, and Contacts
  • Stage every record before it's imported — review what's coming in and uncheck anything you don't want
  • Automatic LOINC matching for lab results
  • Per-batch import history with one-tap rollback if you change your mind
  • Built-in file analyzer shows what's in any CCDA, plus a reference page explaining how to download one from your portal

Know exactly what gets imported

The CCDA Import Catalog shows you, importer by importer, every field PHR pulls in — and what it intentionally skips. The companion Analyze My XML File screen lets you open any CCDA read-only and see what's in it before you commit to importing. No guessing about what made it in or what got dropped.

Medications

Track all your medications — prescriptions, over-the-counter, and supplements. Manage dosages, refill schedules, and check for potential drug interactions.

  • Active and discontinued medication tracking
  • Dosage schedules (daily, weekly, as needed, every-other-day)
  • Refill details with pharmacy information
  • FDA drug interaction checker
  • Printable weekly medication checklist with complex dose timing — morning, evening, twice daily, as needed, and more

Lab Results

Record and monitor lab tests over time. PHR automatically evaluates results against normal ranges and highlights values that need attention.

  • Automatic high/low/normal evaluation
  • Color-coded indicators for quick review
  • Panel-based organization (CBC, metabolic, lipid, etc.)
  • Customizable normal ranges
  • Historical trending across dates

Questions for Your Provider

Keep a running list of things you want to ask your doctor or pharmacist. Tap the question icon on any medication, condition, or lab result to start a new question linked to that record. Then print the list before your visit — with or without the answers you've collected — so the conversation stays focused.

  • Each question is linked to the record it's about, so context is one tap away
  • Status, priority, provider, and linked-visit fields keep larger lists organized
  • Sidebar badge shows how many questions are still open at a glance
  • Print report groups questions by provider and priority, with a one-line-per-question layout
  • Optional Ask AI path drafts a starting answer you can edit, save, and bring to the visit

Ask AI

Got a question about a medication, lab result, or condition in your record? Tap Ask AI on the Question screen and PHR sends your question to the AI service you've chosen. Perplexity opens in Safari and doesn't require an account; Claude, OpenAI, Gemini, and xAI (Grok) are sent directly from the app using your own API key (each requires a paid subscription with that vendor — PHR doesn't provide AI service itself).

  • Pick the AI service you prefer — or turn the feature off entirely
  • Attach a PDF, image, or text file with your question (Claude path)
  • Plain-English warning explains what's leaving the app the first time you use it, and again every time you attach a file
  • Saved answers are stamped with the model and timestamp so you know what produced them
  • Built-in disclaimer reminds you to confirm anything important with a licensed clinician

Health Check

Ask an AI to look at your record without giving up your privacy. Health Check builds a de-identified snapshot — age, sex, height, weight, plus whichever clinical categories you choose to include — and sends it to the AI service you've configured. The result comes back as a PDF attached to a Health Check record so you can save it, print it, or share it.

  • No name, date of birth, address, phone, email, or record numbers ever leave the device
  • Confirmation screen shows exactly what's about to be sent before you tap Send
  • Optional question field for things you specifically want addressed
  • Past Health Checks list keeps every run with its date, model, cost, and PDF for reference
  • Use Claude, OpenAI, Gemini, or xAI (Grok) — your own API key, your own subscription

Powerful Report Writer

Build custom health reports by selecting exactly the sections you need. Choose from 16 data categories, apply filters per section, and generate a single comprehensive document — perfect for doctor visits, travel, or your own records.

  • Select any combination of 16 health data sections
  • Per-section filters: date range, status, type, and more
  • Lab results matrix with color-coded high/low indicators
  • One-tap Travel Health Summary for trips
  • One-tap iPhone Summary — phone-sized PDF to keep in your pocket for emergencies
  • Save as paginated PDF or export to Excel (.xlsx)

Owner Tools — Backup, Transfer, and Audit

The Owner of a PHR device gets a set of system tools for keeping the data safe and portable.

  • Backup — Save a complete copy of every patient's database and all attachments to a folder you pick. Configure two backup locations and PHR writes to both whenever you back up.
  • Restore — Pull a backup back in. PHR compares table by table so you can see what's about to change before you commit, and one tap undoes the last restore if you change your mind.
  • Transfer Patient — Move a single patient's records from one iPad to another via AirDrop or the Files app. Useful when setting up on a new iPad or handing family record-keeping over.
  • Export to Excel — Hand off your records as an .xlsx file with one worksheet per section, frozen header rows, and matching column titles. Useful when sharing with a clinician who works in spreadsheets.
  • System Log — A running audit trail of important actions (logins, backups, imports, AI calls). Good for spotting if something unexpected happened.

For Every User — Help, Reference, and Audit

A few helpers in Settings that every user can reach, regardless of whether they're the Owner.

  • Setup Guide — A walk-through for getting started with the app, plus pointers to the backup and import features.
  • Contact Support — Tap once to open a pre-filled email to support. The message is sent from your own email account, so no credentials leave the app.
  • Support Tickets — Every support email you send is auto-saved so you can refer back to what you asked.
  • LOINC Reference Table — Browse the bundled catalog of 678 lab test codes (across 25 panels) used to standardize lab result names. Search by name, code, or panel — handy when comparing reports from different labs.
  • Login Audit — A history of your own login activity for peace of mind.

Apple Health Import

PHR can pull your records straight out of Apple Health. Choose Apple Health as the source on an import screen, and PHR reads the data your iPhone has gathered from your providers and devices — staging everything for your review before a single record is saved.

  • Brings in clinical records from your providers — lab results, conditions, medications, allergies, immunizations, and procedures
  • Also captures health metrics you track yourself — blood pressure, heart rate, pulse oximetry, and weight — as Vitals
  • Step-by-step iPhone export instructions built into the screen — print them if you want a checklist to take with you
  • Stage and preview everything before anything is saved
  • Duplicate detection means re-importing won't double up records you already have
