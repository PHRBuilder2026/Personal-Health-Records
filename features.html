Features

A comprehensive personal health record — 18 modules to manage every aspect of your health.

Your Complete Personal Health Profile

The foundation of your record — who you are and the baseline details every provider asks for, organized so nothing gets lost.

  • Keep the essentials in one place: blood type, height, weight, age, and preferred language
  • Flag what matters in an emergency — organ donor, DNR status, and advance directive
  • Organize it across tabs: Basics, Contact Info, Emergency, Lifestyle, History, and Notes
  • Track fields providers ask about, like veteran status and military/VA ID
  • Blur sensitive fields on screen and keep them off printed reports with privacy controls

Import from Apple Health

If your conditions are already in Apple Health, PHR brings them in for you — no retyping.

  • Works straight from an Apple Health export — the .zip from Health → Export All Health Data on your iPhone or iPad; PHR unzips and reads it for you
  • Pulls the conditions your doctors have sent to Apple Health — the Health Records you've linked under Settings → Health (not what's typed into your Medical ID)
  • Pick the ordering provider once — Apple's export leaves it out — and PHR stamps it as the source on every condition
  • Name-safety check first: an exact patient match proceeds, a close match asks, a different last name stops the import
  • Review and uncheck anything before it imports, with merge-duplicate rules so repeat imports don't pile up

Every Medication, Organized and Cross-Checked

Keep prescriptions, over-the-counter meds, and supplements in one list — filtered, sorted, and checked for interactions.

  • Filter by status and type — active vs. discontinued, Rx vs. OTC — with running counts
  • Track dose, strength, frequency, quantity, and time of day for each medication
  • Check your whole list for potential drug interactions in one tap
  • Print a weekly checklist that handles complex timing — morning, evening, twice daily, as needed
  • Save custom column views so the list shows exactly what you care about

See Every Lab Result in Context

Record lab tests over time and let PHR flag what's outside range, grouped by panel so trends are easy to follow.

  • Automatic high / low / normal flags that surface values needing attention
  • Results grouped by panel — CBC, metabolic, lipid, thyroid, and more
  • Reference ranges and goals shown right beside each result
  • Graph any test to watch it trend across dates
  • Filter by date range and generate a shareable report

Questions for Your Provider

Keep a running list of things you want to ask your doctor or pharmacist. Tap the question icon on any medication, condition, or lab result to start a new question linked to that record. Then print the list before your visit — with or without the answers you've collected — so the conversation stays focused.

  • Each question is linked to the record it's about, so context is one tap away
  • Status, priority, provider, and linked-visit fields keep larger lists organized
  • Sidebar badge shows how many questions are still open at a glance
  • Print report groups questions by provider and priority, with a one-line-per-question layout
  • Optional Ask AI path drafts a starting answer you can edit, save, and bring to the visit

Ask AI

Got a question about a medication, lab result, or condition in your record? Tap Ask AI on the Question screen and PHR sends your question to the AI service you've chosen. Perplexity opens in Safari and doesn't require an account; Claude, OpenAI, Gemini, and xAI (Grok) are sent directly from the app using your own API key (each requires a paid subscription with that vendor — PHR doesn't provide AI service itself).

  • Pick the AI service you prefer — or turn the feature off entirely
  • Attach a PDF, image, or text file with your question (Claude path)
  • Plain-English warning explains what's leaving the app the first time you use it, and again every time you attach a file
  • Saved answers are stamped with the model and timestamp so you know what produced them
  • Built-in disclaimer reminds you to confirm anything important with a licensed clinician

Health Check

Ask an AI to review your health record — with your privacy built in.

  • Choose exactly which categories to include — conditions, labs, vitals, meds, and more — with the cost estimated before you run
  • No name, date of birth, address, phone, email, or record numbers ever leave the device
  • A confirmation screen shows exactly what's about to be sent before you tap Send
  • Add an optional question for anything you specifically want addressed
  • Every run is saved with its date, model, cost, and PDF for reference
  • Bring your own key for Claude, OpenAI, Gemini, or xAI (Grok)

Powerful Report Writer

Build custom health reports by selecting exactly the sections you need. Choose from 16 data categories, apply filters per section, and generate a single comprehensive document — perfect for doctor visits, travel, or your own records.

  • Select any combination of 16 health data sections
  • Per-section filters: date range, status, type, and more
  • Lab results matrix with color-coded high/low indicators
  • One-tap Travel Health Summary for trips
  • One-tap iPhone Summary — phone-sized PDF to keep in your pocket for emergencies
  • Save as paginated PDF or export to Excel (.xlsx)

Owner Tools — Backup, Transfer, and Audit

The Owner of a PHR device gets a set of system tools for keeping the data safe and portable.

  • Multiple settings for the Owner
  • Handles up to 10 people
  • Backup/Recovery/ Transfer are complex and designed to prevent data loss
  • System Log records key events for review
  • Transfer a patient to a different device
  • Setup your personal AI service

For Every User — Help, Reference, and Audit

A few helpers in Settings that every user can reach, regardless of whether they're the Owner.

  • <strong>Setup Guide</strong> — A walk-through for getting started with the app, plus pointers to the backup and import features.
  • <strong>Contact Support</strong> — Tap once to open a pre-filled email to support. The message is sent from your own email account, so no credentials leave the app.
  • <strong>Support Tickets</strong> — Every support email you send is auto-saved so you can refer back to what you asked.
  • <strong>LOINC Reference Table</strong> — Browse the bundled catalog of 678 lab test codes (across 25 panels) used to standardize lab result names. Search by name, code, or panel — handy when comparing reports from different labs.
  • <strong>Login Audit</strong> — A history of your own login activity for peace of mind.

Document Conditions the Way Your Doctor Does

Keep a complete picture of every diagnosis — symptoms, status, severity, family history, and the standardized codes clinicians use.

  • Capture the details that matter: status, severity, diagnosis date, symptoms, and body system
  • Store the exact ICD-10 and SNOMED codes so your record speaks your care team's language
  • Note family history right on the condition, with room for who and how it runs in the family
  • Link the medications you take for it, plus the providers who diagnosed and manage it
  • Ask a built-in question or generate a shareable report for any condition in a tap

Every Medication, Down to the Imprint

Track prescriptions and over-the-counter meds with the dose, schedule, and identifying details you'd otherwise have to dig for.

  • Record strength, route, frequency, quantity, and whether to take it with food
  • Identify any pill by its description and imprint code, so there's no guessing
  • Look up official drug information from the NIH without leaving the screen
  • Link each medication to the condition it treats and the provider who prescribed it
  • Track status and duration — active, ongoing, or stopped — at a glance

Enter Lab Results Without the Guesswork

Type in the numbers from your report against standardized test panels, so every value lands in the right place with the right units.

  • Start from ready-made LOINC panels — like a CBC with Differential — or build your own
  • Enter results with reference ranges and units already set for each test
  • See at a glance which values fall outside their normal range
  • Tag each result's ordering provider and facility so you know where it came from
  • Skip what you don't need — blank rows are ignored on save

Readings That Tell the Full Story

Track systolic, diastolic, and pulse together, with the details clinicians ask about — where, which arm, and what position.

  • Capture each reading with side, cuff location, and body position for true apples-to-apples trends
  • Flag readings that fall outside the normal range you set
  • Send readings straight to your Vitals with one export
  • Import past readings and graph the trend over any date range
  • Ask a built-in question about what your numbers are showing

Log Blood Sugar with Context That Counts

Record every glucose reading with the timing and circumstances that make the numbers meaningful to you and your doctor.

  • Tag each reading fasting, pre-meal, post-meal, bedtime, or random so patterns stand out
  • Set your own target limits and see when a reading falls outside them
  • Import readings in bulk instead of typing them one by one
  • Graph your trend over any date range
  • Ask a built-in question about your readings whenever something looks off

One Record for the Whole Family

Keep separate health profiles for everyone you care for, each with its own data, all under one account you control.

  • Add a profile for every family member and switch between them in a tap
  • Set who's an owner and who's a user, and transfer ownership when you need to
  • Keep each person's records in their own database, managed separately
  • See each profile's last login and data size at a glance
  • Protect everything with an automatic sign-off after a set period of inactivity
