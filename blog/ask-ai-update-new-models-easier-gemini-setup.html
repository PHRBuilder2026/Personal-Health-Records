Ask AI Gets an Update: New Models and Easier Gemini Setup

Current Models, No Extra Work

AI companies retire and replace their models often, and some of the ones PHR used were getting old. PHR 2.4 switches every AI service to its current models, updates the setup steps, and takes care of the change for you.

What changed

  • New defaults for every service. Google Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite, Claude Haiku 5.5, GPT-6 Luna, and Grok 4.3. If you were using an older model, PHR moves you to its replacement automatically. For Claude, that's about ten times cheaper.
  • Healthcare Mode uses Claude Opus 5.5.
  • More accurate cost estimates. The cost shown on each AI answer now matches each company's current prices.

The free option got easier to set up

Google's free tier on its Gemini models is still the easiest way to try Ask AI at no cost. Google changed its sign-up steps, so PHR's directions now match: sign in at aistudio.google.com with a personal Google account, accept the terms, and choose Create API key. Paste it into Settings → Owner → AI Service Setup and you're answering questions in minutes.

For more on the free option, see Unleash the Power of AI — For Free.
