A Fresh Look for Every Record Screen

Your Records, Easier to Read at a Glance

Every record screen in PHR now has the same clean layout: the name up top, its status as a badge, and the details grouped into cards that each answer one question. It's easier to scan, and what you need first is where your eye lands.

How the new layout works

Open any record — a medication, a condition, a visit, an insurance policy — and you'll see the same pattern. The record's name sits on its own line under the buttons, with tags beside it for things like status, type, or a follow-up date. Below that, the details are split into cards, usually in two columns: who, what, when, and where, each in its own box. If you use large text, the cards stack into a single column so nothing gets squeezed.

The fields themselves haven't moved out of reach. Tap Edit and everything is right where the card showed it.

Small touches that save a step

  • Phone numbers and email addresses are live. On Care Team, Facilities, Insurance, and Contacts, tap a number to call or an address to start an email.
  • Visits flag what's overdue. A follow-up tag shows when you're due back — green while it's coming up, red once the date has passed.
  • Insurance has a "Show at the counter" card. The Member ID and other numbers a pharmacy or front desk asks for are together in one place, with a badge showing whether the policy is Active or Expired.
  • Allergies stand out when they matter. Severity shows as a badge, and a life-threatening allergy gets a red outline on its treatment card.
  • "Used by" lists. A provider or facility record shows which of your other records mention it.

A few screens got more than a new layout

Immunizations now have a Next due card for the following dose, and you can attach documents — a photo of a vaccination card, for example. Legal documents have a Who to call card listing each agent with their phone number. Implants keep their safety details, like MRI compatibility, front and center for imaging appointments.

Where to find it

There's nothing to turn on. Update to PHR 2.4 and open any record. Every screen also has its own Help button — the small ? in the upper-right — if you want a tour of what each card holds.
