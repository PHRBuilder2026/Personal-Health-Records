Welcome to the Track Runner Blog

A Place for Track Runner News

This is the first post on the Track Runner blog — a spot for tips, short how-to videos, and a closer look at new features as they land. It's a test run, so feel free to publish it, open it on your iPad, and make sure everything reads the way it should.

Where to find it

Open the app and tap Blog at the top of the sidebar. New posts show up at the top, and anything you haven't read yet gets a little New marker plus a count on the sidebar.

What you can expect here

  • Tips — small things that make the app quicker to use day to day.
  • How-to videos — short clips when a feature is easier to show than to describe.
  • Feature deep-dives — the why and how behind something new.

That's the whole idea: keep it short, keep it useful, and keep it close to where you're already working.
