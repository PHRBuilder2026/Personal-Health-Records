Run your whole model railroad — on iPad and Mac.

Download on the App Store

For iPad and Mac. Everything stays on your device — no account, works offline.

One app for the whole layout

Model Railroad Record Keeper keeps everything about your railroad in one place: operating jobs with step-by-step session instructions, a full locomotive roster, your layout's locations and tracks, an inventory of rolling stock and supplies, a to-do list, and a library of reference manuals and links. Print clean reports for any of it. No account, no cloud, works fully offline — your railroad lives on your device.

What's inside

Operating Jobs

Build a master job list with step-by-step instructions, assigned locomotives, and start/end locations and tracks.

Locomotive Roster

Road number, model, manufacturer, DCC/sound, availability, photos, and the full F1–F28 function-key map for every engine.

Locations & Tracks

Catalog the locations on your layout and the tracks at each one, plus the structures and devices that live there.

Inventory

Rolling stock, structures, electronics, tools, and scenery — with categories, storage locations, value tracking, reorder reminders, and a for-sale flag.

To-Dos & Reference

A task list with priorities and time tracking, plus a library of manuals and reference links — including PDFs kept on your device.

Reports & Printing

Clean, native reports for your roster, job list, locations, and inventory — print over AirPrint or export to PDF.

A look inside

Your data stays yours

There's no account to create and nothing is uploaded to a server. The app works with no internet connection, and built-in backup and restore lets you save and move your data whenever you want. See our Privacy Policy for the full details.
