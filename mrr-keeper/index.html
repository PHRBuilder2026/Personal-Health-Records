One app for the whole layout

Model Railroad Record Keeper keeps everything about your railroad in one place — operating jobs, a full locomotive roster, your layout's locations and tracks, inventory, to-dos, and a library of reference manuals. Print clean reports for any of it. No account, no cloud, works fully offline: your railroad lives on your device.

Your Locomotive Roster

Every engine on your railroad at a glance — searchable, sortable, and printable.

  • Road number, model, manufacturer, DCC or DC, sound, and availability
  • The full F1–F28 function-key map and a photo for every locomotive
  • Build operating jobs with step-by-step instructions and assigned engines
  • Filter, sort, and print your roster and master job list

Map Your Whole Layout

Organize the railroad the way you actually operate it — every location with its tracks, buildings, and devices.

  • Locations with their tracks, structures, and on-site devices
  • A full inventory of rolling stock, structures, electronics, tools, and scenery
  • Storage locations, value tracking, reorder reminders, and a for-sale flag

Reference, To-Dos & Reports

Keep the paperwork and the next-steps right where you need them.

  • Manuals, wiring diagrams, and reference links — including PDFs kept on your device
  • A to-do list with priorities, categories, and start/finish time tracking
  • Clean, native reports for any section — print over AirPrint or export to PDF
  • Built-in backup & restore, so your data is always safe and portable

Your data stays yours

There's no account to create and nothing is uploaded to a server. The app works with no internet connection, and built-in backup and restore lets you save and move your data whenever you want. See our Privacy Policy for the full details.
