Model Railroad Record Keeper

Run your whole model railroad — on iPad and Mac.

One app for the whole layout

Model Railroad Record Keeper keeps everything about your railroad in one place: operating jobs with step-by-step session instructions, a full locomotive roster (road number, model, manufacturer, DCC/sound, function keys, and photos), your layout's locations and tracks, an inventory of rolling stock and supplies, a to-do list, and a library of reference manuals and links. Print clean reports for any of it.

Everything stays on your device — no account, no cloud, works fully offline. Built-in backup and restore keeps your data safe and easy to move.
