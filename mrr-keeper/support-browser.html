Support

Help and answers for the browser version — Windows, Android tablets and Chromebooks.

Using the App Store version on iPad or Mac? Support for that version is here.

Need a hand?

The best way to reach us is right inside the app. Choose the envelope in the banner to open Contact Support — it opens a message with the screen you were on already filled in, so we can help faster. Write what’s going on and send it.

Contact Support hands the message to your own email program, addressed to us and pre-filled with what you typed. Nothing is sent until you send it, and it goes from your own email account — so our reply lands in your inbox and the whole exchange sits in your sent mail. Every request gets read.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Model Railroad Record Keeper?

It’s an all-in-one app for documenting and organizing your model railroad. Keep your operating jobs and step-by-step session instructions, a full locomotive roster (road number, model, manufacturer, DCC/sound, function keys, photos), your layout’s locations and tracks, an inventory of rolling stock and supplies, a to-do list, and a library of reference manuals and links. It also prints clean reports.

What does it run on?

Windows, Android tablets and Chromebooks — anywhere you have a current version of Chrome or Edge. You want a decent-sized screen: the tables, rosters and reports are built for room.

How do I install it?

Open the app’s address in your browser and choose Install when it offers — in Chrome and Edge that’s an icon at the right-hand end of the address bar, or Install in the browser menu. It lands on your desktop and Start menu like an ordinary program and opens in its own window.

You can also just use it in a browser tab. Installing is worth it though: you get the window, the icon, and dependable offline behaviour.

Does it work without an internet connection?

Yes. Once installed, the app runs offline — your railroad is on your machine, not on a server. The one time it needs the internet is when you enter your licence key, to check the key is real. After that it stops asking.

Where is my data stored?

On your machine, in a private storage area your browser sets aside for this app. There is no account and nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Be aware of what that storage is, though: it belongs to the browser. Clearing this site’s data removes it, and a browser short on space is entitled to reclaim it. No browser promises to keep it forever. That is why the backups below matter.

How do I back up my railroad?

Open Backup and choose Back Up Now. You can nominate a folder for the app to write backups into, or take a plain download to wherever your browser puts files. Back up regularly, and always before you change machines or clear browser data.

How do I move my railroad to another machine?

Install the app on the new machine, open Backup, and restore from your backup file. You’ll see a summary of what’s in the file before anything changes, and you can choose to replace everything or merge it with what’s already there.

How does buying it work?

You buy from our shop and get a licence key. Enter the key in the app once and it’s yours for good — no expiry, no renewal, no subscription. Until a key is entered, the app opens read-only with a demo railroad loaded, so you can look around properly before you decide.

I lost my licence key.

Use Contact Support and tell us the email address you bought with, and we’ll get you sorted.

Is my information private?

Yes. The app collects nothing, and nothing about your railroad leaves your machine. The single thing it ever sends is your licence key, once, to check that it is real. See our Privacy Policy for the full details.
