Custom Views: See Your Roster Your Way

11/24/2026

On the App Store

Your lists, laid out the way you work

Every list in Model Railroad Record Keeper starts with a standard set of columns. Custom Views let you save your own layouts instead: just the columns you care about, in the order you want, showing only the rows you need. They're on Locomotives, Rolling Stock, Inventory and Todos.

Make a view

Tap View, then Manage Views and Add. Give it a name — "Weathering", "For Sale", "BNSF by Car Type" — then pick the columns you want and drag them into order. A few identifying columns always stay first, so you can tell what each row is.

Make as many views as you like. Standard is always there to switch back to, and switching views never changes the filters at the top of the screen.

Show only what you need

Under Show only rows where, add a condition: a column, a test, and a value. Some examples:

  • Weathered is Yes — every car you've weathered
  • Road contains BNSF — catches "BNSF" and "BNSF Railway" alike
  • Sold is No — just the things you still own

The tests are is, is not, contains, is blank and is not blank. "Is" needs the exact text; "contains" finds it anywhere. Add more conditions and choose All of these or Any of these. The screen's own filters still apply on top, and a green "View filter" line tells you one is in effect.

Sort two ways

Sort by one column, then another. Sort Rolling Stock by Car Type, then by Road, and you'll see every boxcar together, ordered by road, then the covered hoppers, and so on. Tapping a column header still changes the first sort.

Print what you see

Print → Current View prints the list just as it's shown — your columns, your filters, your sort. When a view has a second sort, the printout starts a new section for each value of the first: a "Car Type: Boxcar" heading, its cars, then the next type.

Keep track of your views

Manage Views lists each one with its column count, and notes when it has a filter or its own sort. Edit changes it, and Delete removes the layout — never your records. Views ride along in your backups.
