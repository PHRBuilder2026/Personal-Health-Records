Yard Office How-To Videos, Right from Help

11/22/2026

On the App Store

Learn the Yard Office by watching

The Yard Office now has seven short how-to videos, one for each part of the job. You can watch them right from the app's Help, or on our website.

The videos

  • Overview — a quick tour. Start here.
  • Car Location — put your cars on the layout.
  • Routes — set up a train run once.
  • Orders — what each industry wants set out and picked up.
  • Quick Switch List — a fast list of cars to move, no orders needed.
  • Build Train — plan the train and print the switch list.
  • Dispatch — key in what the crew did, then Complete the train.

Finding them in Help

Tap the ? on any Yard Office screen. Under the description you'll see How-To Videos — tap one and it plays on our website. The Help Index also has a How-To Videos button that opens the full list.

On the website

The videos page lists them all, newest first. On the Features page, look for the green Video button next to a feature.

The videos have no ads.
