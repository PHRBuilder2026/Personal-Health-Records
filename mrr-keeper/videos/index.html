How-To Videos

Short videos showing how to use Model Railroad Record Keeper, one task at a time.

On the App Store
▶ Introduction to the Screen A quick tour of the parts every screen shares: the left panel and its submenus, the icon that hides the left panel, the To-Do (wrench), Support (envelope) and Help icons, the action buttons, the filters, and the screen ID. ▶ Inventory How to keep track of your inventory: the Inventory list, adding and editing items, storage locations, the Inventory Pivot, and setting up your inventory lookups. ▶ Custom Views How to make your own Custom Views on Locomotives, Rolling Stock, Inventory and Todos: choose and order the columns, show only the rows you want, sort them, and print exactly what's on screen. ▶ Yard Office: Overview A tour of the Yard Office: where your cars are, routes and orders, Quick Switch Lists, building a train, and keying in what happened on Dispatch. Start here. ▶ Yard Office: Build Train How to build a train: pick a route, plan it from your orders or a Quick Switch List, print the Switch List, then key in what happened on Dispatch. ▶ Yard Office: Car Location How to put your cars on a location and track, one at a time or a batch at once, and see what is sitting where. Car Location is where operations start. ▶ Yard Office: Dispatch How to key in what happened after a session: every line starts Done, mark the ones the crew didn't finish as Not Done with why and where the car ended up, then Complete the train. ▶ Yard Office: Orders How to set up orders: each one is an industry asking for the cars it wants dropped and the cars it has ready to go. A train fills them, then they move to history. ▶ Yard Office: Quick Switch List How to make a quick list of cars to move, each going from where it sits now to where you send it, with no routes or orders to set up. To run it, pick the list on Build Train. ▶ Yard Office: Routes How to set up a route once: where the train starts and ends, the industries it serves in order, and its locos. Each session a train picks the route on Build Train.
