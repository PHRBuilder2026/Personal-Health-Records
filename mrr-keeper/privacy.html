Privacy Policy

Model Railroad Record Keeper — last updated September 11, 2026

Model Railroad Record Keeper is developed by Evans & Associates PHR, LLC ("we," "us"). The short version: the app does not collect, transmit, or share any of your data. Nothing about your railroad is ever sent anywhere.

Where your data is kept

Everything you enter — your locomotive roster, operating jobs, locations, inventory, to-dos, reference links, photos, and PDFs — stays on the machine you entered it on. We do not operate a server, we do not have accounts, and none of it is sent to us or to anyone else.

It is stored on your device like any other app’s data, and it stays there.

No analytics, no tracking, no advertising

The app includes no analytics, advertising, or any tracking technology. This website uses Google Analytics to count visits to its pages — that is the website, not the app, and it sees nothing you enter into the app.

Backups are under your control

The app exports a backup file and lets you choose where it goes. We never see these files; they are written by your machine, onto your machine. Restoring a backup reads a file you select.

You pick a destination your device offers, such as iCloud Drive, and your device’s own system backups may also include the app’s data — governed by Apple’s privacy policy, not ours.

Photos and camera

If you attach a photo to a record, the app stores that image with the record and nowhere else. On a tablet you will be offered the camera; on a desktop, a file picker. Photos are not uploaded anywhere.

Reference links

The app includes a built-in browser for finding and saving reference links for your models. You are dealing with those websites directly, under their own privacy policies, and the app neither records nor transmits your browsing activity.

Contacting support

If you use the in-app Contact Support feature, the app opens your email program with a message addressed to us. An email is sent only if you choose to send it, from your own email account, and will include whatever you write plus basic context (such as the screen you were on). We use that email solely to respond to you.

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and post the revised version at this page.

Contact us

Questions about this policy? Open Contact Support inside the app and we'll help.
