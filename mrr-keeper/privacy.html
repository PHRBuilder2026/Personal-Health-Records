Privacy Policy

Model Railroad Record Keeper — last updated September 6, 2026

Model Railroad Record Keeper is developed by Evans & Associates PHR, LLC ("we," "us"). It comes in two versions: the app from the App Store, and the browser version you install from the web. This policy covers both. The short version: neither one collects, transmits, or shares any of your data. The browser version sends your licence key to the shop once, to check it; that is the only thing either version ever sends anywhere, and it says nothing about your railroad.

Where your data is kept

Everything you enter — your locomotive roster, operating jobs, locations, inventory, to-dos, reference links, photos, and PDFs — stays on the machine you entered it on. We do not operate a server, we do not have accounts, and none of it is sent to us or to anyone else.

In the App Store version it is stored on your device like any other app’s data. In the browser version it is kept in a private storage area your browser sets aside for this site. It never leaves your machine, but it is the browser’s storage: clearing the site’s data removes it, and a browser short on space is entitled to reclaim it. No browser promises to keep it forever. That is why the backups below matter more in the browser version than they do in the App Store one.

No analytics, no tracking, no advertising

Neither version of the app includes analytics, advertising, or any tracking technology. This website uses Google Analytics to count visits to its pages — that is the website, not the app, and it sees nothing you enter into either version.

Backups are under your control

Both versions export a backup file and let you choose where it goes. We never see these files; they are written by your machine, onto your machine. Restoring a backup reads a file you select.

In the App Store version you pick a destination your device offers, such as iCloud Drive, and your device’s own system backups may also include the app’s data — governed by Apple’s privacy policy, not ours. In the browser version you can nominate folders for the app to write backups into, or take a plain download to wherever your browser puts files.

Photos and camera

If you attach a photo to a record, the app stores that image with the record and nowhere else. On a tablet you will be offered the camera; on a desktop, a file picker. Photos are not uploaded anywhere.

Reference links

The App Store version includes a built-in browser for finding and saving reference links for your models. In the browser version, saved links open in a new tab of the browser you are already using. Either way you are dealing with those websites directly, under their own privacy policies, and the app neither records nor transmits your browsing activity.

Licence keys

The browser version is sold with a licence key. When you enter your key, the app sends that key once to the shop that issued it, to check that it is real, and remembers the answer — it does not ask again. That is the only time the app contacts anyone on your behalf. Your key goes; nothing about your railroad does. The App Store version has no licence key and makes no such check.

Contacting support

If you use the in-app Contact Support feature, the app opens your email program with a message addressed to us. An email is sent only if you choose to send it, from your own email account, and will include whatever you write plus basic context (such as the screen you were on). We use that email solely to respond to you.

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and post the revised version at this page.

Contact us

Questions about this policy? Open Contact Support inside the app and we'll help.
